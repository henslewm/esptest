| R-001 | Chat or model state diverges from repository | Medium | High | Conflicting summaries | Startup reads and closeout updates | Project owner | Open |

| R-005 | BLE HID pairing with Windows 11 is finicky | Medium | Medium | Pairing fails or device does not reconnect | Scope BLE to a single keyboard HID input report; one-time pairing then auto-reconnect | Project owner | Open |
| R-006 | Device-driven Notepad macro is timing- and focus-sensitive | Medium | Medium | test.txt not created or keystrokes land in wrong window | Pre-opened-editor fallback path | Project owner | Open |
| R-007 | Compile/simulation success may diverge from real hardware behavior | High | High | Firmware builds but does not behave on-device | VERIFIED_ON_HARDWARE earned only by operator attestation, never authored | Project owner | Open |
| R-008 | Flashing over USB-Serial/JTAG persists that USB across reset, so the TinyUSB HID composite does not activate until a one-time unplug/replug (OTG replug-to-activate) | Medium | Low | HID composite absent after a JTAG-mode flash until the device is physically replugged | Documented in `docs/HARDWARE_BASELINE.md` (ADR-004); one-time unplug/replug after a JTAG-mode flash; `tools/flash.py` buttonless path; will investigate smoothing the behavior | Project owner | Open |
| R-009 | ESP32 BLE advertising can interfere with other nearby 2.4GHz/BLE peripherals (observed: dropped the user's Logitech M720 Triathlon BLE mouse while the board BLE was live) | Medium | Medium | A nearby BLE peripheral drops or will not reconnect while the board is advertising | Low TX power (`ESP_PWR_LVL_N3`), slow advertising (~200-250ms interval), and stop advertising on connect (ADR-006, `ble_keyboard.cpp`); keep the board away from the BT receiver during testing | Project owner | Open |
| R-010 | Firmware timestamps derive from build-time `__DATE__`/`__TIME__` + uptime with no RTC, so they drift over time and reset to build time on every reboot | Medium | Low | `test.txt` timestamps diverge from wall-clock or restart from the build time after a power cycle | Accepted per ADR-001 (lowest-cost constraint, no WiFi/NTP/credentials); an RTC or WiFi/NTP sync would be the alternative if accurate wall-clock time is later required | Project owner | Open |
